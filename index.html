Who we fit them for

The hardware is much the same. What changes is who needs to get in, how often, and who is handing out the access.

Homes

Prints for the family, a code for the house help, and no more spare key with the neighbour.

Apartments

One app across every unit. Add a tenant, remove a tenant, no locks to change between them.

Hotels & Airbnbs

A code that works Friday to Sunday and dies on Monday. No meeting guests at midnight to hand over keys.

Businesses

Cards for staff who change often, and a record of who opened which door and when.

Gate automation

Gates that open before you reach them.

We automate the gate you already have. Motor, arms, wiring and remotes, fitted in a day on most compounds — and it still opens by hand when the power goes.
