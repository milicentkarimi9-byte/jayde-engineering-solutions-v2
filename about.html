About us

We started with one door.

Jayde Engineering Solutions fits smart locks, cameras and automatic gates across Nairobi. We also let two furnished apartments in Westlands, which is a separate side of the business.

Most people meet us because something about getting into their own building annoys them.

A spare key living with a neighbour. A tenant who lost theirs again. A watchman being woken up at eleven to open a gate. A cleaner who can only come when someone is home to let her in. None of these are dramatic problems. They are just small daily frictions that nobody thinks to fix, because the lock on the door has looked the same for fifty years.

We started fitting digital locks because they solve that quietly. You stop handing out metal and start handing out permission — a fingerprint for the people who live there, a code for the people who visit, and the ability to take either one back without changing a single lock.

Anyone can order a lock online. Whether it fits your door, whether the bolt meets the frame, and whether somebody answers the phone in six months — that is the part worth paying for.

That is why we did not stop at selling hardware. We fit what we sell, and the difference between a lock that works and one that annoys you every day is almost always the fitting rather than the box it came in.

So we repair locks we did not sell, and we set up the app with you before we leave rather than leaving you the manual. Cameras and automatic gates came next because the same customers kept asking. Solar is where we are heading.

Separately, we let two furnished apartments in Westlands as short stays. Different business, same city, same phone number.
