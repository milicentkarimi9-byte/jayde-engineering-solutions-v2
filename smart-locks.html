What we actually do

Most people need one of these four. Plenty need all of them, in this order.

  • 01 / Supply

    Selling the lock

    Six locks in stock from KSh 17,999 to KSh 32,100, plus face-recognition models on request.

    • Send a photo of your door and its thickness, we tell you what fits
    • Written price before anything is ordered
    • Wooden, metal, sliding and aluminium doors all covered
  • 02 / Fitting

    Custom fitting

    Under two hours on most doors. Fitting is already in the price on several locks.

    • Old hardware out, alignment checked, nothing cut until it lines up
    • Every entry method tested before we leave
    • Your prints and codes loaded with you watching
  • 03 / Repairs

    Fixing locks that stopped behaving

    We repair locks we did not sell you. Bring us the problem, not the receipt.

    • Reader not picking up a finger it used to know
    • Batteries draining in weeks instead of months
    • Door dropped, bolt no longer meeting the frame
    • Codes not working after a power cut
  • 04 / App

    Tuya setup and troubleshooting

    The app is where the lock becomes useful. We set it up properly rather than leaving you the manual.

    • App installed, lock paired, Wi-Fi joined
    • Members added, temporary codes explained
    • Lock offline or not responding remotely? We sort it
    • Several units on one account for landlords

Five ways into the same door

Every lock we fit takes all five, and you decide who gets which. A code for the house help. A fingerprint for your tenant. The app for yourself. The key stays in a drawer for the day the power and the Wi-Fi both go.

Fingerprint

Up to a hundred fingers per lock.

Passcode

Permanent, one-time, or expiring on a date you choose.

IC card

A tap card for staff who change often.

Mechanical key

Real keys and a real cylinder, every time.

Tuya app

Open it from the shop, from work, from Mombasa.

How a fitting goes

Under two hours on most doors, from arrival to a working lock with your prints and codes already loaded.

  • Step one

    We look at the door

    Send photos and the thickness, or we come and look. Either way you have the price in writing before anything is ordered.

  • Step two

    We fit and test it

    Old lock out, new one in, alignment checked. Before we pack up we test every way of opening it, the mechanical key included.

  • Step three

    We hand it over properly

    App on your phone, prints and codes enrolled with you watching, and a number that answers afterwards.

OUR INSTALL

One app for every door you own

Our locks run on Tuya Smart. A landlord with six units manages all of them from one screen instead of six sets of keys. We install the app on your phone before we leave and add the first codes with you watching.

  • Open the door from anywhere

    A delivery arrives while you are at work. You unlock from your phone, they leave the parcel inside, you lock it again.

  • Codes that expire on their own

    Set a code that works Friday to Sunday. By Monday morning it is dead, and you never had to remember to change it.

  • A record of every entry

    The app keeps a list of every time the door opened and which fingerprint or code did it. Landlords tend to find this the most useful part.

  • An alarm if someone forces it

    Try to pry the lock and it screams at the door while sending you a notification at the same time.

  • Warning before the battery dies

    You get warnings for weeks before the batteries go flat. Nobody gets locked out of their own house.

Questions we get asked

  • Will it fit my door?

    Most likely. We fit wooden, metal, sliding and aluminium doors. Send a photo and the thickness in millimetres and we will tell you before you pay anything.

  • What happens when the power goes?

    Nothing. The lock runs on batteries, not mains. You also keep real keys and a real cylinder, so there is always a way in.

  • How long does a fitting take?

    Under two hours on most doors, including setting up your app and enrolling your prints.

  • Can I add people myself afterwards?

    Yes, and we show you how before we leave. Adding a fingerprint or sending a code takes under a minute in the app.

  • What if it stops working?

    Call us. There is a twelve month warranty on the hardware, and we repair locks we did not sell you as well.

  • Do you only work in Nairobi?

    Nairobi and the surrounding counties. Ask us about anywhere else — we travel for larger jobs.
